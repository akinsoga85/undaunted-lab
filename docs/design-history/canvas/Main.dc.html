Step 01 · Research & positioning · Oct 2026

undauntedlab.com — redesign brief

Undaunted Lab now runs five live or near-live products across three countries, but its website still shows three earlier projects and a contact form. The redesign should present the lab as one credible studio that ships AI-native tools for safety and access.

What the current site gets wrong

01 · Outdated portfolio
It shows Maiite, Ganusii and Ward IX. Scamfendr, NotisCare, Nestlo and Vervet are missing.
02 · Off-message
The site says "projects that connect us." The lab now stands for safety, access and AI at the core.
03 · No way in
There is no navigation, and no product has a link out. A form is the only call to action.
04 · No proof
The site has no founder story, no explanation of how the lab builds, and no sign that the products are live.

Each product already has its own look: Ganusii is dark green and golden, NotisCare is navy and compliance-led, Nestlo is warm cream, and Vervet is a cream document. The lab's site needs a neutral, confident frame so that each product's own colour stands out inside it.

Positioning

Undaunted Lab builds AI-native tools that protect everyday people and open the doors systems keep shut.

Built in the UK, Nigeria and Canada, by a founder with a background in financial crime.
Messaging pillars
SafetyCatch harm early: fraud before payment, decline before a hospital transfer, compliance gaps before an audit.
AccessMake the hard parts of life simple: arriving in a new country, getting to work, getting your money back.
AI at the core, honestlyEvery product is built around AI, and each one is clear about what the AI does and what it doesn't. Vervet's rule that AI drafts explanations but never makes detections is a strong proof point.

Audiences and what each needs

Partners & investorsA clear thesis, proof that products ship, the founder's edge, and a contact route.
Product usersFind "their" product in one click: by country, by problem, or by name.
Talent, press & collaboratorsHow the lab works, what it is building next, and the founder's writing on LinkedIn.

Patterns from strong studio and lab sites

  • Lead with the portfolio, and treat products as the hero rather than a gallery.
  • Show a live status for each product, such as live, pilot or in development.
  • Give the founder a voice: a short, signed note beats an "About us" page.
  • Explain how the lab builds in three or four principles rather than a services list.
  • Give each audience one call to action: partner, try a product, or follow the work.

Design principles for the redesign

  1. Show it working. Every product appears with a real UI moment, such as a check, a triage or a plan.
  2. One lab, many brands. A neutral frame with each product's colour inside it.
  3. Motion with meaning. Animation should show protection, signals and journeys, never decoration alone.
  4. Fast and inclusive. Respect reduced motion, meet AA contrast, work on mobile first, and load quickly on Lagos networks.
