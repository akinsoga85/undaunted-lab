// 003 — how we build

Four rules. No exceptions.

01AI at the coreEvery product is designed around a model from day one. AI is never bolted on afterwards.
02Humans make the callAI drafts, sorts and flags. A person signs off, and the product is clear about which is which.
03Built where people areWhatsApp in Lagos, Telegram in the UK, offline in a care home hallway. There are no app stores to get through.
04Ship, then sharpenReal pilots with real users before polish, and the evidence decides what grows.
