Step 02 · Wireframe · shared by all three directions

One homepage, twelve beats

The page has a single scrolling story with routes out for each audience. All three directions share this structure. What changes between them is the visual language and the signature interaction.

01 · NAV: Products · How we build · Lab notes · Founder · [Partner with us]
SIGNATURE VISUAL
terminal / shield network / persona story
02 · HERO: positioning line, one-line proof, two CTAs (Explore products / Partner with us)
03 · PROOF STRIP: 5 products · 3 countries · UK · Nigeria · Canada (moving ticker)
04 · MANIFESTO: 2–3 lines, highlighted word by word on scroll
05 · PORTFOLIO: filter by problem or country, 4 live products, each card links out to its own site
LIVE PRODUCT MOMENT
a check, a triage, a match, a plan
06 · PRODUCT SHOWCASE: tabs swap an animated UI moment for each product (the main interactive section)
07 · HOW WE BUILD: 4 principles (AI at the core · humans decide · local first · ship and learn)
08 · IN THE LAB: Vervet (in development) plus 2 unnamed "coming soon" experiments, with a notify-me option
09 · FOUNDER NOTE: Opeyemi, from financial crime to building, with a signature
10 · LAB NOTES: latest LinkedIn essays on AI productivity and financial safety
11 · CONTACT: three routes (Partner or invest · Build with us · Press), each opening a short form
12 · FOOTER: products, LinkedIn and X, "still naija." sign-off kept as a nod to heritage

Interaction rules
  • Portfolio becomes a horizontal swipe on mobile.
  • Showcase tabs become a segmented control.
  • All motion pauses under prefers-reduced-motion.
  • Hero visual works without a cursor (touch and keyboard).
  • Every product card is a real link to its live site.
Later pages (phase 2)
  • /products/[name]: a case-study page for each product
  • /lab: principles, stack and open roles
  • /notes: essays and announcements
